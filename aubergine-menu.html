Kericho Cuisine Stop

Founded in 2018, Aubergine Restaurant came onto the Kericho dining scene with a bang.

Here, the food is honest and intelligent, focused on using only the freshest and locally available ingredients. A quick look at the menu confirms this philosophy.

There’s an assortment of starters to choose from including their signature Sweet and Sour Chicken wings, Smoked Sailfish, and Beef Carpaccio, as well as an assortment of crisp salads, velvety soups, and delicious house sandwiches.

Seafood is delivered fresh daily from Kenya’s coast using refrigerated transport to give us sublime dishes such as Swahili Coconut Fish, and Chilli Prawns, while the local organic beef, duck, and chicken, sourced from the farms around Kericho yields succulent and flavorful dishes such as the Kericho Beef Fillet, Pan Roasted Duck in Orange Sauce, and Lemon and Rosemary Chicken. If you’re after some casual dining Aubergine also offers an extensive pizza menu from the classic Margherita to the child favourite Hawaiian.

All prices are in Kenya Shillings and include value added tax and catering levy.
